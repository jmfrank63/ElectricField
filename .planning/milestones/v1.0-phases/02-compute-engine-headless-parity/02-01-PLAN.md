## Phase Goal

**As the** developer, **I want to** have a cancellable threaded compute engine with headless SVG/PNG output, **so that** CI can verify every preset on all OSes without a display.

This plan delivers the engine: a preset now computes completely (field lines, equipotentials, de-duplication, Bezier fit) on N threads and can be cancelled or destroyed within the 200 ms contract for every preset and method. Plan 02-02 then pins the CORE-07 regressions and race-checks the engine under ThreadSanitizer.

<objective>
Port the MFC compute path into the std-only core as a RAII, cancellable, deterministic `ComputeSession` (D-01..D-04) with the equipotential tracer, the verbatim de-duplication `Finalizer` (D-05) and the guarded Bezier fit (D-07, D-08 parity kept), harden the method lookup (D-15), and prove determinism, per-preset invariants, de-dup, poll, cancellation latency for every preset and every method, destructor join, the stress scenario and the boundary cases with doctest.

Purpose: CORE-04..CORE-06, QA-02 and the 1-vs-N / cancel-latency half of QA-03 are the numerical and threading contract every later layer (ef-cli, CI parity, Phase 3 GUI) builds on.
Output: 8 core files, 2 test files, build.zig source lists; two commits.
</objective>
