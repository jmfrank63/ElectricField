## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** set options, keep them between runs and export images, **so that** the app fully replaces the MFC version.

This plan lands the options half: the std-only options model with validation and ini persistence, the View ▸ Options… dialog, and the immediate-repaint vs new-tabs semantics. Export follows in 04-02; MFC removal in 04-03.
